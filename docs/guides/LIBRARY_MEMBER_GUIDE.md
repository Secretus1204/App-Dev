# Library Member Guide

## Create and secure your account

1. Register with your own name, member ID, email address, and password. Public registration always creates a Library Member account; it cannot create an Admin.
2. Log in with the same email and password. Use **Forgot password** if needed.
3. If the app asks for a temporary-password change, complete it before continuing.
4. Use **Profile** to update only your display name or change your password. Keep your password private.

## Find and request a book

1. Open **Catalog** and search by title, author, ISBN, or category.
2. Open a title to view its details and availability.
3. Choose **Request book** only when the title is requestable. You may have only one pending request for the same title.
4. Check **My Library** for request status, current loans, due date, and loan history.

## Notifications and returns

- Open **Notifications** to see approval, rejection, due-soon, overdue, and return updates.
- Bring borrowed physical books to the librarian for return. The Librarian records the return; the loan then appears in history.
- A rejected request may include a reason. A pending request can be cancelled; approved/loaned requests cannot be cancelled from the app.

## Get help

Contact the library if your account becomes inactive, your member ID/email is incorrect, or a due date/loan record appears wrong. Do not create a second account to work around an account issue.
